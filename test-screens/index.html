SShield

Real work, private data, one AI agent

People in banks, colleges, companies and support desks work on screens full of other people's data, so they aren't allowed to use AI agents. Shield hides that data on the laptop first. Each scenario below is a real job, with a task the agent completes without ever seeing who it's about.

Banking · KYC operations

Approve a customer's KYC

An analyst clears a queue of ten identity checks, each with a name, Aadhaar, PAN, account number and phone.

Hidden from the AI: names, Aadhaar, PAN, account numbers, phones, deposits, the ID card images.

Type or sayApprove the KYC application that has every document verified and low risk.

Open the KYC console
Education · Admissions office

Shortlist from a merit list

Admissions staff fill the last seat from ten applicants, each with Aadhaar, phone, email and a parent's name.

Hidden from the AI: names, Aadhaar, phones, emails, parents' names.

Type or sayShortlist the highest-ranked applicant whose documents are complete and fee is paid.

Open admissions
Company · Payroll

Release one salary

HR releases month-end pay from a list with PANs, bank accounts, IFSC codes and salaries.

Hidden from the AI: names, PAN, bank accounts, IFSC, every salary, the PAN card image.

Type or sayRelease the salary for the engineering employee whose payroll is ready.

Open payroll
E-commerce · Customer support

Reply to a customer
